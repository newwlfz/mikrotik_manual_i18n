Summary of documentation changes made on May 25, 2026.

## Routing

- **[OSPF](/docs/7.24/user-guides/routing-and-networking-protocols/unicast/ospf) documentation split into separate pages** — reorganized from one large page into focused sections: *Understanding OSPF*, *Neighbour Relationship*, *Routing Calculation*, and *Areas & Virtual Links*.
- **[Policy routing](/docs/7.24/user-guides/routing-and-networking-protocols/policy-routing) guide rewritten** — cleaner language and better examples throughout. A warning about routing table resolution was corrected to a tip.
- **[BGP FAQ](/docs/7.24/user-guides/routing-and-networking-protocols/unicast/bgp/faq) updated** — additions and corrections to the BGP frequently asked questions page.
- **[IS-IS](/docs/7.24/user-guides/routing-and-networking-protocols/unicast/is-is) page updated** — small fixes and additions.
- **[Moving from ROSv6 to ROSv7](/docs/7.24/user-guides/routing-and-networking-protocols/moving-from-rosv6-to-rosv7) guide updated** — small corrections to the migration guide.

## Bridging & Switching

- **[Basic VLAN switching](/docs/7.24/bridging-and-switching/user-guides/basic-vlan-switching) guide expanded** — more detail and improved formatting in the basic VLAN switching case study.
- **[CRS1xx/2xx switch examples](/docs/7.24/bridging-and-switching/user-guides/crs1xx-2xx-series-switches-examples) enhanced** — additional content in the CRS series switch configuration examples.
- **[Spanning Tree Protocol](/docs/7.24/bridging-and-switching/user-guides/spanning-tree-protocol) case study improved** — more explanation and better formatting.
- **[WMM and VLAN priority](/docs/7.24/bridging-and-switching/user-guides/wmm-and-vlan-priority) page updated** — small corrections and formatting improvements.
- **[Wireless VLAN trunk](/docs/7.24/bridging-and-switching/user-guides/wireless-vlan-trunk) case study enhanced** — improvements to the wireless VLAN trunk example.

## Wireless

- **[Wireless interface](/docs/7.24/wireless/abgn/wireless-interface) page improved** — general review and cleanup of the legacy wireless interface documentation.
