Summary of documentation changes made on May 22, 2026.

## Authentication & AAA

- **[RADIUS](/docs/authentication-authorization-accounting/radius)**: Clarified the RadSec note — with RadSec enabled, RouterOS forces both the shared secret to radsec and the port to 2083, regardless of what was configured manually.

## Getting Started

- **[CHR installation](/docs/getting-started/installation-and-upgrade/install/chr-installation/)**: Moved the CHR installation guides (VirtualBox, VMware, Hyper-V, Vultr, and the other platform walkthroughs) out of the licensing section and into Installation and Upgrade, where the rest of the installation documentation lives.
- **[RouterOS licensing](/docs/getting-started/routeros-licensing/)**: Continued the licensing restructure — reworked the overview and [MikroTik hardware](/docs/getting-started/routeros-licensing/mikrotik-hardware/) pages now that installation content has moved out.

## Site

- **Sidebar**: Curated the documentation sidebar — section ordering and grouping were reworked, and the [Wireless case studies](/docs/wireless/user-guides/) page became a proper category with its own landing page.
