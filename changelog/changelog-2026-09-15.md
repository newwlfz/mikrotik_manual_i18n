Summary of documentation changes made on September 15, 2026.

## Site

- Fixed the machine-readable Markdown (.md) versions of some pages being missing or listed at the wrong URL in llms.txt; the [Console](/docs/management-tools/console), [WinBox](/docs/management-tools/winbox), and [WinBox Legacy](/docs/management-tools/winbox-legacy) pages now have Markdown files matching their HTML addresses.

## Hardware

- **[CCR2116-12G-4S+](/hardware/ccr2116-12g-4s-plus)**: Added a full specifications table (CPU, RAM, storage, power, ports, certification) and a warning to power down and wait 10 seconds before opening the chassis, for example to install an M.2 SSD. Corrected the port list to 13 Ethernet ports and 4 SFP+ ports.
